# Benchmarks

This directory contains results and configurations for standard LLM benchmarks including GSM8K, ARC-Challenge, and CMT (Custom Math Tasks).

## Supported Datasets

- GSM8K: Grade School Math
- ARC: AI2 Reasoning Challenge
- CMT: Custom mathematical reasoning tasks

## Methodology

- Multiple passes with baseline and modified prompts
- Automated grading and comparison
- Statistical summary generation
- Caching of Hugging Face datasets for reproducibility

Detailed results are available in the `runs/` directory (not committed to git).
